The bench

Old circuits deserve a steady hand

We work from a single shared bench above a record shop, not a queue of technicians passing your gear along a line. Every module that comes in gets opened by the person who diagnosed it, repaired by the person who understands why it failed, and calibrated by ear before it ever ships back.

That means we take fewer jobs than a bigger shop would. It also means the fault gets fixed instead of patched over, and the character of the instrument — the drift, the warmth, the quirks you fell in love with — stays exactly where you left it.

What we take on

Three ways onto the bench

Diagnostic & Repair

Dead voice, stuck knob, or drifting pitch — we trace it to the component and fix only what's broken. Nothing gets replaced on a guess.

Full Restoration

Recapping, re-normalizing jacks, repainting panels. Gear that looks and performs the way it did the day it left the factory.

Custom Builds & Panels

One-off panels, control-voltage mods, and expansions built around your own patching habits, not a stock spec sheet.

How a repair actually moves

The signal chain

  1. 01

    Intake

    Tell us the symptoms in your own words. We log every quirk before we ever open the case.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    We signal-trace the full path, oscillator to output, until we find exactly where it breaks.

  3. 03

    Repair

    Only the failed parts get replaced. The original character of the circuit stays intact.

  4. 04

    Calibrate

    Retune, renormalize, and run a 24-hour burn-in on the bench before we call it done.

  5. 05

    Return

    It ships with a spec sheet showing exactly what changed, and why it changed.
